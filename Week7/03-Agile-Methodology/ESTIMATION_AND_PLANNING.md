# Agile Estimation and Planning

## Story Points

Story points measure relative effort, uncertainty and complexity rather than hours. A common scale is 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 and 13.

## Planning Poker

1. Product Owner explains the story.
2. Team discusses assumptions and risks.
3. Each member selects a card independently.
4. Estimates are revealed together.
5. Highest and lowest estimators explain their reasoning.
6. The team votes again until it reaches a shared estimate.

## Velocity

If the team completes 20, 22 and 18 story points in three sprints, its observed average velocity is 20 points. Velocity is a planning aid, not a performance target.

# Scrum Framework

## Roles

- **Product Owner:** prioritizes value and maintains the product backlog.
- **Scrum Master:** facilitates Scrum and removes impediments.
- **Developers:** create the usable product increment.

## Ceremonies

- Sprint Planning
- Daily Scrum
- Sprint Review
- Sprint Retrospective
- Backlog Refinement

## Artifacts

- Product Backlog
- Sprint Backlog
- Increment
- Definition of Done
